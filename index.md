## About
{: #about .visually-hidden}

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Political Science at Rice University. I develop and apply statistical methods to address measurement and causal inference challenges in the study of partisan polarization.

Most of my current research examines how errors in survey responses affect what we can learn about political attitudes. I am the principal investigator on a National Science Foundation (NSF) CAREER grant supporting the development of methods to address these errors, with applications to measuring support for political violence and belief in conspiracy theories.

I earned my PhD in Political Science from Stanford University in 2021 and hold bachelor's degrees in Statistical Science and Mathematics from Duke University.
